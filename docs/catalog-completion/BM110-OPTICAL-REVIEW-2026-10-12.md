# BM110 reference correction — 2026-10-12

Manufacturer BM110 V2.0 PDF page 26, section 6.3.4, lists top D24.9 × 1.5, middle D27.9 × 4.1 and bottom D27.9 × 4.1 protective windows. Section 5.2.2 / PDF page 19 illustrates the middle cover-glass drawer; PDF page 20 illustrates the compound collimation assembly.

The earlier four-group diagram and v3 optical visualization omitted the middle protective window. The earlier v2 raster had an additional curved lens at this location, which is also not the correct flat cover-window representation. Both simplified interpretations are superseded by this review.

Correct scoped order for the represented optical path: QBH, upper cover window, compound collimation, middle cover window, compound focusing, lower cover window, ceramic/nozzle assembly. Exact hidden drive geometry and detailed manufacturing positions remain outside the evidence available. The corrected volumetric asset is a module visualization, not certified exact internal CAD.
